Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Hermes Personal Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner, Felton Jackson. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts) to help manage schedules, email, and documents.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for. It is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
